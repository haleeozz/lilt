# Hale Builds demo outline

Working title: **Üç Spotify Hesabımı Birleştirmek İçin Kendi Aracımı Yaptım | Lilt**

Target length: 3–5 minutes. This is an outline, not a finished or uploaded video.

1. **Problem (30 seconds):** Music was scattered across old accounts, and transfer services had limits.
2. **Build (60 seconds):** Explain the local app, account consent, source/target identity checks, and read-only sources. Use fake account names and the offline demo.
3. **Result (45 seconds):** Describe the real migration's 981 liked songs and the subsequent 11 themed playlists. Explain that aesthetic grouping and covers were part of the personal workflow and are not included in the current generic release.
4. **Reliability (45 seconds):** Show the interrupted-transfer test resuming without duplicates. Explain how unavailable items are reported rather than silently treated as success.
5. **Lessons and next steps (30 seconds):** Spotify quota limits, experimental status, and the open-source link once published.

Record narration over silent screen footage. Keep OAuth credentials, email addresses, callback queries and keyed local URLs out of the frame. Prefer synthetic examples. No music playback is needed to demonstrate the tool.
